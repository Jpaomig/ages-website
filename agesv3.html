Our Core Expertise

Timely and Reliable Pile Testing Services

PDA (Pile Dynamic Analysis, ASTM D4945), PIT (Pile Integrity test- Low Strain, ASTM D5882), Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL ASTM, D6760) and other pile testing requirements involving micro-piles, bored piles, precast concrete driven pile, and steel piles among others.

Featured Service

Pile Dynamic Analysis (PDA)

ASTM D4945

High Strain Dynamic Test to determine bearing capacity and integrity of piles.

Learn More

Service

Pile Integrity Test (PIT)

ASTM D5882

Low Strain Integrity Testing using handheld hammer and sensor to determine pile integrity and check for major defects.

Learn More

Service

Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL)

ASTM D6760

Integrity testing for bored piles using preinstalled steel tubes on rebar and ultrasonic probes.

Learn More

Service

Static Load Testing (SLT)

ASTM D1143, D3689, D3966

Gold standard pile testing with direct load and settlement measurement to determine bearing capacity.

Learn More

Service

Bi-Directional Testing (BDT)

ASTM D8169

Alternative to static load testing, advantageous for larger test loads with practical site constraints.

Learn More

Service

Plate Load Test (PLT)

ASTM D1195, D1196

Conventional method to determine bearing capacity of ground and subgrade modulus for foundations.

Learn More

Service

Length Inductive Test Equipment (LITE)

For determining lengths of existing piles, this test is effective to estimate the length of existing steel / metal piles. This may be applied in sheet piles, steel pipe piles, H-Piles whose length is unknown.

Learn More

Service

Parallel Seismic Test (PST)

ASTM D8381

For determining lengths of existing concrete piles in driven and bored piles whose length is unknown. Normally applied in bridges and ports.

Learn More

Service

SHaft Area Profile Evaluator (SHAPE)

Advanced borehole inclination and imaging tool. Provides 3D images of holes with increased sensor profiles compared to traditional methods.

Learn More
